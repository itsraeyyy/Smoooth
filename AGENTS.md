# Smoooth Project Guidelines & Design Rules

## 1. Anti-Slop Rule
- **NEVER use decorative pill-shaped eyebrow badges, kicker pills** (`rounded-full`, pastel backgrounds, sparkle icons, uppercase tracking-wide text, status dot pills) above headings, heroes, cards, or sections. Headings must start cleanly and directly.
- Avoid generic AI-generated marketing slop, fake seed round announcements, invented customer logos, and filler buzzwords.

## 2. Design Craft Standard: Less Is More, More or Less
- Grounded in `farming-labs/design-eng-skills` and Emil Kowalski's *Simplicity in the Age of AI*.
- **Removing is harder than adding**: Every element must be intentional. If an addition doesn't make the user's workflow or understanding clearer, take it away.
- **Reserved yet styled**: High-contrast typography (**Instrument Serif** for display, **Geist** for UI, **Geist Mono** from Better Auth for code, and **Silkscreen** for subtle pixelated technical markers).
- **Subtle pixel aesthetic**: Reserved, miniature, industrial dotted accents and glyphs (inspired by Overseer) without crossing into childish cartoon styles.
- **Proof over decoration**: Show real terminal commands, real adapter payloads (PostgreSQL, Redis, Mongo, SQLite), real MCP tool invocations, and verifiable release checksums.
- **Immediate interactions**: Fast, crisp interactive states (default, hover, active/pressed `scale(0.97)`, visible focus ring). No sluggish or distracting decorative animations.
- Full responsive fidelity across desktop (1440px/1280px), tablet (900px), and mobile (390px/360px).
