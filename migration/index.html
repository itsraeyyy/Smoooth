Moving from Coolify or Docker Compose

Migrate production stacks in seconds with zero downtime, automatic database volume safeguards, and over 1.3 GB of RAM instantly reclaimed on your VPS.

Scenario // Solo SaaS & Indie Founders

Eliminate OOM build crashes on $5/mo VPS nodes

Coolify consumes 1.4 GB RAM just idling with PHP, Laravel Horizon, and internal daemons. When Next.js builds trigger on a 2 GB VPS, the Linux kernel terminates your Postgres database.

  • 1.35 GB RAM Reclaimed: Smoooth idles at under 28 MB.
  • Volume Lock: Automatic pgdata retention prevents data loss.
  • Zero-Downtime TLS: Embedded Caddy with sub-second ACME.

# 2. Apply transactional migration
$ smoooth migrate coolify /data/coolify/services/saas-prod --apply

✓ Redis 7 ready (in-memory caching layer active)
✓ Next.js 15 container healthy (HTTP 200 on /)
