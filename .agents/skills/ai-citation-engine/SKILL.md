# AI Citation Engine — Agent Skill

> **Origin:** Tibo's data-backed playbook ([tmaker.io/humans-pay](https://www.tmaker.io/humans-pay)), cross-referenced with 12+ independent research sources. Adapted for ANDebet.
>
> **Core thesis:** AI assistants name the products the web keeps mentioning in the places they read. Those places are mostly pages search engines already show. There is no separate "AEO playbook" — there's just: does your content answer the question better than everyone else, and does the rest of the web confirm it?

---

## Why This Matters — The Numbers That Justify Action

Before executing any step, internalize the landscape:

### The Click Is Dying, The Mention Isn't
- **Impressions 2.18×, clicks only 1.22×** over 10 months (Outrank, 415 sites)
- **8% click rate** with AI summary vs **15% without** (Pew, 68,879 searches)
- **58% lower CTR** for #1 result when AI Overview shows (Ahrefs, 300k keywords)
- **79% of sites** lost click-through rate (Outrank)

### AI Visitors Are Extremely Valuable
- **0.5% of traffic → 12.1% of signups** = ~23× conversion rate (Ahrefs, own site)
- **4.4× value** of an organic visitor (Semrush, modelled)
- **1.13B referrals** from AI platforms to top 1,000 sites in June 2025, +357% YoY (Similarweb)

### The Audience Is Massive
- **9.5B visits/month** to gen-AI platforms (Similarweb)
- **2B monthly users** on Google AI Overviews (Alphabet Q2 2025)
- ChatGPT's share falling (76% → 53%); Gemini and Claude growing

## The 5-Lever Framework

Everything in this skill maps to these 5 levers. **Levers 1–2** are what you say about yourself. **Levers 3–5** are what the web says about you.

### Lever 1: Make Your Product Super Niche
**You control this. No tool needed.**

- [ ] Define one sentence: **"The [X] for [Y]."** Put it on homepage title, meta description, About page, social bios, and the first line of every guest post
- [ ] Publish the definitive category page ("what is [your niche], and how to choose one")
- [ ] Create comparison pages: "[You] vs [Competitor]" for each meaningful competitor
- [ ] Create alternatives pages: "[Competitor] alternatives" for each bigger competitor
- [ ] Ship one free tool (calculator, checker, analyzer) — AI can summarize your definition, it can't run your calculator

**Data backing:** Free tools get **2.11× CTR** vs articles; "what is" definitions get **0.52×** (Outrank, 6.2M pages, 3,332 sites). Being niche narrows the competition for AI answer slots.

### Lever 2: Be the Best in Your Category
**You control this. Nothing to buy.**

- [ ] Generous free tier or trial — make it easy for AI to recommend trying you
- [ ] Lots of people saying it's good publicly — G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit, tweets
- [ ] Specific, checkable claims — "Median 2.1 days from order to published" beats "fast"
- [ ] Honest limits — say when your product isn't the best fit

### Lever 3: Get Into Listicles
**What the web says about you. Hard by hand.**

- [ ] Run your prompt set (see Measurement below) and collect every listicle cited
- [ ] Prioritize ones ranking on Google AND Bing for "best [category]"
- [ ] Offer owners something real: free account, reader discount, original data
- [ ] Track placements and monitor them monthly

**Data backing:** **43.8%** of ChatGPT-cited pages are "best X" lists (Ahrefs); **21.9%** of all citations are listicles, **40% of commercial queries** cite listicles (Wix Studio, 1.06M citations).

### Lever 4: Get Links & Mentions from Sites People Actually Read
**A link inside a real article = a brand mention. That's the part that matters.**

- [ ] Earn contextual links on sites with real Google traffic (DR 30+, 100+ clicks/mo)
- [ ] Guest posts with genuine value, not footer links on dead sites
- [ ] Digital PR, original data, case studies that earn organic links

**Data backing:**
- Branded web mentions correlate **0.664** with AI visibility vs backlinks at **0.218** (Ahrefs, 75k brands)
- YouTube mentions are the **strongest single correlate** of AI visibility (~0.737, Ahrefs)
- DR 30+ sites get **29× the AI citations** of <DR 10 (Outrank, 200 sites)
- Google impressions are the **strongest predictor** of AI citations (0.80 correlation, Outrank)

### Lever 5: Get on Reddit
**Assistants look for real opinions. Reddit threads rank on Google. Google feeds AI.**

- [ ] Identify subreddits where your buyers ask questions
- [ ] Build genuine karma by contributing useful answers
- [ ] Reply usefully in threads ranking for your category keywords
- [ ] Never start with promotion; earn trust first

**Data backing:**
- Reddit is the **4th most visible domain** in Google US (SISTRIX)
- **#1 source for Perplexity** (6.6% of citations) and AI Overviews (2.2%) (Profound)
- Even post-drop, "Reddit and Wikipedia are still ChatGPT's two most-cited domains" (Semrush)

### Beyond the 5: Additional Channels
- [ ] **YouTube** — Short explainers and comparisons; get reviewed by creators (~0.737 correlation)
- [ ] **Wikipedia/Wikidata** — Ensure facts about your category/company are accurate (#1 cited domain for ChatGPT, 7.8%)
- [ ] **Review sites** — G2, Capterra, Trustpilot; ask happy customers for reviews
- [ ] **LinkedIn, Quora, Q&A forums** — Feed AI search like Reddit, smaller scale

## Measurement Protocol

> **Rule:** You can't improve a share of AI answers you've never looked at.

### Initial Baseline (Days 1–7)

1. **Write 20–30 buyer prompts** — Category questions, NOT your brand name:
   - "Best [category] tool"
   - "[Competitor] alternatives"
   - "How do I [job your product does]"
   - "[Category] for [specific use case]"
   - "[Competitor A] vs [Competitor B]"

2. **Run each prompt 5–10 times** across:
   - ChatGPT
   - Perplexity
   - Gemini
   - Copilot
   - Google (check for AI Overviews and AI Mode)

3. **Record for each run:**
   - Were you named? (mention)
   - Were you linked? (citation)
   - Which pages were cited? (the listicles/threads you need to be on)
   - Which competitors were named?

4. **Calculate baseline metric:** "Named in X of N runs" per prompt

5. **Build gap list:** For each lost prompt, identify why:
   - No page answering that question?
   - Not on the cited listicles?
   - Nobody on Reddit mentioned you?

### Technical Prerequisites Checklist

- [ ] **robots.txt** allows: OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Bingbot, Googlebot
- [ ] Site verified in **Bing Webmaster Tools**, sitemap submitted
- [ ] **IndexNow** configured for new pages
- [ ] Main content served as **HTML** (AI crawlers don't render JavaScript — Vercel data)
- [ ] **Google Search Console** connected and tracking
- [ ] Analytics segmenting referrals from: chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com
- [ ] "How did you hear about us?" added to signup flow

### Ongoing Tracking (Monthly)

- [ ] Re-run all prompts 5–10 times
- [ ] Compare "named in X of N" to previous month
- [ ] Monitor Google impressions weekly (leading indicator)
- [ ] Track AI referral traffic and conversions
- [ ] Refresh content that's slipping (AI cites 25.7% fresher pages — Ahrefs)

## 90-Day Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Measure (Days 1–7)

**Objective:** Know where you stand and where the gaps are.

```
Output: Baseline spreadsheet with:
- 20–30 prompts × 5–10 runs × 5 platforms
- "Named X/N" per prompt
- Gap list with root cause for each lost prompt
- List of every listicle/thread cited by AI for your prompts
- Technical prerequisites all checked
```

**Agent actions:**
1. Audit robots.txt for AI crawler access
2. Check Bing Webmaster Tools verification
3. Verify SSR (no JS-only content)
4. Audit existing content against buyer prompts
5. Generate the 20–30 prompt list based on product positioning
6. Document competitor mentions across all platforms

### Phase 2: Own What You Control (Days 7–30)

**Objective:** Fill every gap on your own site.

- [ ] Finalize and deploy one-sentence niche positioning
- [ ] Publish answer page for **every prompt you lost**
- [ ] Create comparison pages for top 5 competitors
- [ ] Create alternatives pages for top 3 bigger competitors
- [ ] Ship one free tool/calculator for your category
- [ ] Begin daily publishing cadence
- [ ] Add structured data (FAQ, HowTo, Product schema)
- [ ] Add quotations, statistics, and cited sources to key pages (Princeton GEO: these help lower-ranked sources get cited)

**Content quality rules (from Princeton GEO paper):**
- Add real quotations from experts/customers
- Include specific statistics with sources
- Cite external sources
- Do NOT keyword stuff (it hurts)
- Gains are largest for pages that start lower

**Leading indicator to watch:** Google impressions climbing

### Phase 3: Get Talked About (Days 30–60)

**Objective:** Make the web mention you.

- [ ] Pitch the listicles that AI cited for your prompts
- [ ] Offer each listicle owner: free account, reader discount, original data
- [ ] Pursue contextual links from sites with real Google traffic
- [ ] Begin useful Reddit participation in your category's subreddits
- [ ] Ask happy customers for reviews on G2, Capterra, Trustpilot
- [ ] Create YouTube explainers/comparisons for your category
- [ ] Publish original data/research others will cite

**Expected reality:** Most listicle owners won't respond. Tibo pitched 400+ and got 1 placement. Persistence and offering genuine value is the only path.

### Phase 4: Compound (Days 60–90)

**Objective:** Measure progress and double down on what works.

- [ ] Re-run all prompts and compare with Phase 1 baseline
- [ ] Refresh any pages that slipped in rankings
- [ ] Increase investment in specific lists, sites, and threads that got you named
- [ ] Update all comparison/alternatives pages with latest features
- [ ] Publish case studies from early customers

**Realistic targets at 90 days:**
- First citations in Perplexity and Copilot
- Named in some runs for a handful of prompts
- Google impressions visibly climbing
- Judge the full plan at 6 months, not 6 weeks

## Decision Trees

### "Should I pursue this channel?"

```
Is my buyer on this platform?
├── No → Skip for now
└── Yes
    ├── Am I already being cited there? → Double down, don't fix what isn't broken
    └── Am I NOT being cited?
        ├── Is there a page answering this question on my site?
        │   ├── No → Write the page (Lever 1)
        │   └── Yes
        │       ├── Am I on the listicles being cited?
        │       │   ├── No → Pitch listicle owners (Lever 3)
        │       │   └── Yes
        │       │       ├── Are real sites linking/mentioning me?
        │       │       │   ├── No → Earn links and mentions (Lever 4)
        │       │       │   └── Yes → Check Reddit/YouTube/Reviews (Lever 5)
        │       │       └── ...
        │       └── ...
        └── ...
```

### "Which AI assistant should I prioritize?"

```
Perplexity → Most citations by volume (41.6%), shows results first
Copilot → Second most (19%), Bing-powered
Google AI Mode → 13%, on Google's index
Gemini → 12%, growing user base
AI Overviews → 10%, 2B monthly users
ChatGPT → 4.8% citations but biggest audience, hardest to crack
→ Answer: Measure ALL. Prioritize where your buyers are.
```

### "Is my content good enough for AI?"

```
Does it directly answer a specific question? (not "about us" fluff)
├── No → Rewrite to answer the specific question
└── Yes
    Does it include specific, checkable claims? (numbers, dates, comparisons)
    ├── No → Add statistics, quotations, cited sources
    └── Yes
        Is it fresher than what's currently cited? (AI cites 25.7% fresher pages)
        ├── No → Update with latest data
        └── Yes
            Are other sites saying the same thing about you?
            ├── No → That's the real gap. Work Levers 3–5
            └── Yes → You're likely already getting cited. Keep going.
```

## Content Publishing Playbook

### What to Publish (Priority Order)

1. **Answer pages** — One per lost buyer prompt. Title = the prompt. Answer = your genuine take
2. **Comparison pages** — "[You] vs [Competitor]" with honest pros/cons for each
3. **Alternatives pages** — "[Big Competitor] alternatives" with your product included fairly
4. **Free tools** — Calculators, checkers, analyzers (2.11× CTR vs articles)
5. **Category definitions** — "What is [your niche]" with your expertise woven in
6. **Original research** — Data only you have; others will cite it
7. **Case studies** — Named customers, real numbers, honest about bad months

### Content Format Priority (by Google CTR multiplier)

| Priority | Format | CTR vs articles |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Free tools & calculators | **2.11×** |
| 2 | Comparisons & alternatives | 0.88× |
| 3 | Guides & tutorials | 0.80× |
| 4 | How-to articles | 0.80× |
| 5 | Question articles | 0.79× |
| 6 | "Best X" listicles (your own) | 0.69× |
| 7 | "What is" definitions | 0.52× |

### Page-Level Optimization (Princeton GEO Paper)

For every key page:
- [ ] Include 2–3 real quotations from experts or customers
- [ ] Include 3–5 specific statistics with sources
- [ ] Cite 3+ external authoritative sources
- [ ] Do NOT keyword stuff
- [ ] Focus optimization effort on pages currently ranked lower (gains are largest there)

## Known Risks & Honest Caveats

1. **No one can guarantee an AI mention.** Answers are unstable — <1 in 100 chance of the same brand list twice (SparkToro, 2,961 runs)
2. **Paid links carry Google risk.** Google spam policies classify buying links for ranking as spam. Use rel="nofollow" or rel="sponsored" for paid placements
3. **Reddit prohibits inauthentic participation** and vote manipulation. Only post genuinely useful content from your own account
4. **Volume alone may not matter.** Ahrefs found almost no relationship between page count and AI visibility. What matters is pages that each answer a real question
5. **Results take months.** Outrank's data: +7% month 1, +27% months 2–3, 2.0× by month 7. Judge at 6 months, not 6 weeks
6. **One in four sites don't grow.** 24.4% of Outrank's 1,383 sites lost clicks. 8.2% lost half or more
7. **All vendor data is correlational.** Including Outrank's. Treat any single number as a direction, not proof
